Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026 • Version 2.4 (Strict Financial Privacy Standard)

🛡️ 1. Our Fundamental Privacy Promise

At ZenBudget, we believe that your personal finances are strictly your business. We never sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal financial records to advertisers, insurance agencies, credit bureaus, or third parties under any circumstance.

ZenBudget operates on local-first device encryption paired with private encrypted cloud vaults for multi-device sync.

📂 2. What Data We Collect & Why

  • Account Identification: Email address and authentication tokens via Supabase Auth to enable multi-device sync and subscription status restoration.
  • Financial Logs (Self-Reported): Expense categories, budgets, income logs, and envelope allocations inputted manually or via OCR scans.
  • AI Camera & Receipt OCR Scans: Images captured through the AI Smart Scanner are processed transiently in memory to extract vendor names and amounts, then stored locally on your device vault.
  • Device & Analytics Telemetry: Anonymized crash logs and app performance telemetry to improve stability and uptime.

🔒 3. Data Storage & Military-Grade Encryption

All sensitive financial data is protected using AES-256 bit device-level encryption and TLS 1.3 in-transit encryption. When enabling Biometric App Lock (Fingerprint / Face ID), authentication is handled via native hardware keystores on iOS and Android with zero key exposure.

💳 4. Banking & UPI Payment Security

ZenBudget utilizes Direct UPI Intent Deep Linking for scan-and-pay transactions. ZenBudget never asks for, views, or stores your UPI PINs, NetBanking passwords, or CVVs. Subscription billing is processed via Cashfree Payments (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateway).

🗑️ 5. Data Deletion & Export Rights

You have full ownership of your data. You can:

  • Export: Download your entire transaction ledger as CSV or JSON anytime from Settings.
  • One-Click Permanent Deletion: Instantly wipe all local data and cloud database records with zero lingering backups via the Reset & Delete Account tool.

📬 6. Privacy Inquiries & Support

If you have any questions regarding your data privacy or rights under digital data protection guidelines, contact our privacy compliance officer directly at hello.zenbudget@zohomail.in.
