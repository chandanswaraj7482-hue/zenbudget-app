Privacy Policy 🌿

Last updated: August 2026

Welcome to ZenBudget. Your privacy and financial security are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our web application, PWA, or mobile APK.

1. Data Collection & Privacy First

ZenBudget operates on a strict privacy-first architecture. All your daily financial entries, budgets, transactions, and custom categories are stored securely within your device's native local storage and encrypted cloud database.

2. Payment & Scan & Pay Information

ZenBudget uses Direct Deep Linking (Intent Redirection) for peer-to-peer payments. We do not store your UPI PINs, bank account passwords, or debit/credit card details on our servers. Subscription and unlock payments are handled via Cashfree's PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway.

3. Security

We implement 256-bit encryption standards, biometric locks (Fingerprint/Face ID), and secure local sandboxing to ensure your financial records remain completely private and inaccessible to third parties.
