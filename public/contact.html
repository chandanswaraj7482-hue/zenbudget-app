Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my lifetime or yearly subscription?
Simply sign in with the exact same email address used during checkout. ZenBudget will automatically verify your license from the encrypted cloud vault.
Can I use ZenBudget on iPhone / Mac?
Yes! ZenBudget is a modern standalone PWA. On iPhone, tap Safari's Share button [↑] → "Add to Home Screen". On Mac, click "Add to Dock" in Safari or the Install icon in Chrome.
Are my bank credentials stored?
Never. ZenBudget operates with local-first encryption and direct UPI intent deep links. We never store or ask for banking passwords or UPI PINs.
