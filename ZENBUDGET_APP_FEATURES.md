### 🧘 2. Budgeting & Wealth Engineering
- **Safe Daily Pace Calculator**: Live calculation of exact safe daily spend (`(Income - Savings Goal - Fixed Bills) / Remaining Days`) to eliminate end-of-month cash crunches.
- **50/30/20 Rule Budgeting**: Auto-categorizes cashflow into **Needs (50%)**, **Wants (30%)**, and **Savings/Investments (20%)**.
- **Category Caps & Visual Progress**: Individual budget limits for Food, Shopping, Bills, Transport, Entertainment, etc., with color-coded warnings.
- **🗓️ New Month Salary & Budget Prompt**: Auto-check popup at the start of every month asking *"Did your monthly salary or budget change?"* with 1-tap edit or "Keep Same" options.
- **Wealth Compound Simulator**: Interactive 5, 10, and 20-year projection tool modeling long-term compound growth of your savings.
